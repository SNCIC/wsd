Inter-Company Trade Agreements

Two-way documents, kept in line

One trade agreement links two companies of the database, with one switch per direction, so both companies can sell to each other at the same time.

  • A confirmed sale order creates the purchase order of the buyer.
  • An approved purchase order creates the sales order of the seller.
  • A posted customer invoice creates the vendor bill of the buyer.
  • A validated delivery fills the receipt of the buyer.

The agreement also says how far the two documents follow each other: changes of editable counterparts are pushed, cancelling the source document cancels or flags the counterpart, and deleting a document deletes the free counterpart or is refused when the other side is still open.

Every synchronization is logged, conflicting changes are detected from the previous snapshot, and the inconsistencies found by the daily check land in a queue of issues to replay or handle.
